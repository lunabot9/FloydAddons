# Floyd Addons 5.5

## Added
- Searchable block and mob lists with model previews, plus a live Cone Hat preview in settings.
- Companion conversations, optional gameplay comments, and smoother speech preparation and playback.

## Fixed and improved
- Improved Auto Experiments startup, focus handling, and reopening the Experimentation Table between games.
- Corrected Chronomatron and Ultrasequencer reward tracking and Ironman experience-bottle consumption.
- Improved Superpairs scouting, immediate matching of known priority rewards, and Instant Find choices. Priority is ultra-rare books, rare items, then other books.
- Improved equipment/player-head texture compatibility and transparency.
- Kept custom fonts active for the scoreboard and inventory HUD.
- Protected existing settings from being overwritten after an incomplete configuration load.
- Added a matching sidebar divider above Edit HUD and improved companion rendering efficiency.

## Downloads
Choose the JAR matching Minecraft **26.1**, **26.1.2**, or **26.2**. **Fabric API** and **Fabric Language Kotlin** are required.

All three targets passed their builds and automated checks. Live-client verification used Minecraft 26.1.2. SHA-256 checksums are attached alongside the three runtime JARs.
