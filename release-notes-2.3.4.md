## FloydAddons 2.3.4

### Highlights

- Added a new `Low Poly Tung` bundled player model, including the bundled OBJ/texture asset, first-person arm rendering, and held-item alignment for that model.
- Added the `Rotate-Only Swing` animation option so held items can stay planted while still rotating during swings.
- Added a clickable `Buy Me A Coffee!` link under the FloydAddons title in the V2 click GUI and below the community links in the fullscreen Floyd GUI.
- Synced the fullscreen Floyd GUI community links so `github` and `.gg/FLOYD` share the same chroma cycle.

### Included technical work

- Added the living-entity renderer accessor and low-poly model plumbing needed for bundled first-person rendering.
- Updated legacy click-GUI runtime verification coverage for the new animation toggle and new community-link behavior.
- Added third-party notice coverage for the bundled `Low Poly Tung` source asset.
